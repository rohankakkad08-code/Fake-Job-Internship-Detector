      🛡️ Safety Tips

      🏦

      Never Pay to Apply

      Legitimate employers never ask for registration fees, deposits, or training costs upfront.

      📧

      Check Email Domains

      Real companies use official email domains (e.g. hr@company.com), not Gmail or Yahoo.

      🔗

      Verify on LinkedIn

      Search the company on LinkedIn/Glassdoor before applying or sharing personal documents.

      📞

      Demand Proper Interviews

      Any job that skips the interview process and hires immediately should raise red flags.

      🔏

      Guard Personal Info

      Never share your Aadhaar, PAN, SSN, or bank details before a verified offer letter.

      📊

      Research Salary Norms

      Use Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, or Payscale to check if the offered salary is realistic.
